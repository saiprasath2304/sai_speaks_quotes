# Privacy Policy for Sai Speaks

Last updated: [25/02/2026]

Sathya Sai Speaks (the "App") is a non-commercial, devotional application designed to provide daily inspiration through quotes.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The App does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

### Data Stored Locally
- **Favorites**: When you mark a quote as a favorite, it is stored locally on your device's internal storage using `SharedPreferences`. This data is not shared with us or any third parties.

### Internet Access
- **Quote Fetching**: The App connects to GitHub (via `raw.githubusercontent.com`) to fetch the latest quotes. 
- **Privacy Policy**: The App may provide a link to this privacy policy.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The App may use the following third-party services:
- **GitHub**: Used for hosting and fetching quote text.
- **Google Fonts**: Used for application typography.

These services may collect technical information (like IP addresses) as part of their standard operation. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

## 3. Data Safety
We do not share your data with any third parties. Your "Favorites" remain on your device unless you choose to share them using the built-in share functionality (which Uses the system's native share dialog).

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
